Support

Jersey Arrivals; last updated 12 November 2026

Jersey Arrivals shows live bus arrivals and light rail times for NJ TRANSIT. Write to vnallapps@gmail.com with any question, bug or request. One person reads that address and will answer.

The app says there are no stops near me

It covers New Jersey and the NJ TRANSIT routes that run into New York. From anywhere else, nothing will be nearby. Search for a stop by name or by the number on the stop's pole instead.

A bus is on the list but not on the map

NJ TRANSIT does not report a position for every bus. When a bus is missing the app says so under the map, and names which arrival it means. The time shown for it still comes from NJ TRANSIT; it is just not something we can draw a pin for.

Light rail shows times but no trains

NJ TRANSIT publishes live positions for buses only. Light rail times in the app are the timetable, and the app labels them as such.

The times are wrong

Every time comes from NJ TRANSIT and the app does not adjust them. When NJ TRANSIT gives a prediction the app shows it; when there is only a timetable, the app says scheduled. If a bus never arrives, the data was wrong before the app saw it. For service problems, NJ TRANSIT is the place to ask.

Finding a stop number

It is printed on the bus stop pole. You can also search by street name and pick from the list.

Privacy

Nothing is collected. The privacy policy says so in full.

Jersey Arrivals is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by NJ TRANSIT. Arrival data is provided by NJ TRANSIT.
