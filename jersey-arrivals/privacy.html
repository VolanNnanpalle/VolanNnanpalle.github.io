Privacy Policy

Jersey Arrivals; last updated 12 November 2026

Jersey Arrivals collects nothing about you. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, and no advertising. The app contains no third-party tracking or advertising software of any kind.

Your location

If you allow it, the app reads your location to find NJ TRANSIT stops near you. That search happens on your phone, against a list of stops that is already stored on your phone. Your location is never sent to us, never sent to anyone else, and never stored anywhere but in the memory of the running app. You can refuse the permission and the app still works; you search for stops by name or number instead.

What is stored on your phone

The stops you save, and your settings. They stay on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Arrival times

Arrival times come from NJ TRANSIT. The app requests them through a server we operate, which exists so that the app does not call NJ TRANSIT directly. That server receives the stop number being looked up and nothing that identifies you or your device. It keeps no record of who asked, and it stores no personal information.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Since the app collects nothing, we do not expect it to.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or anything else, to vnallapps@gmail.com.

Jersey Arrivals is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by NJ TRANSIT. Arrival data is provided by NJ TRANSIT.
